Recommendation: Integrum, with the Keystone mark

Integrum
  • It names the buyer's job. The people who sign for this are asset-integrity managers. Integrum is the Latin root of integrity.
  • It names what the app does. The same root gives integrate: every capture of an asset brought into one intact record.
  • It has weight. Three syllables, Latin, hard consonants. It sits beside Palantir and Lattice without copying them.
  • It has the cleanest screen of the strong names. The only notable holder is a medical-device maker in another class. getintegrum.com is free.
  • It needs no UI repaint. Its mint accent is the same hue as the app's current accent.

If you want the shortest, hardest name, take Fusor, but launch it as "Fusor by Synapse" until it owns its search results. If you want plain English with the timeline story, take Syncline.

Method and limits

  • Names came from the PRD's capabilities, not from myth. Names from rounds 1 to 3 were excluded. On your instruction, Arabic names were removed from the round.
  • Each name had two or three web searches against software, drones, geospatial, reality capture, inspection, oil and gas, defence and AI, and against both app stores. The search engine is US-centred and recall is thin, so "none found" does not mean clear.
  • Domains were checked live with RDAP on 7 Oct 2026 (Verisign for .com, Identity Digital for .ai and .io). Every bare .com on the list is registered, so each finalist shows a free fallback. Raw results are in rdap-results.txt.
  • Marks are hand-placed SVG on a 64-unit grid, and each works in one colour. Wordmarks are set in Google Fonts for this board. The chosen one would be redrawn as outlines.
  • Next steps: you pick one; counsel clears it in classes 9 and 42 in the US, EU and GCC; we secure the fallback domain and handles; then packages/brand is updated. Stratlas stays the working name until then.
