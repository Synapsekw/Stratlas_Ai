Synapse Solutions · Naming round 2 · 3 Oct 2026

Stratlas mark, refined, and names that fit it

The founder chose the stacked-layers idea from round 1. This round refines that mark six ways: 3, 4 or 5 layers; isometric plates, flat bands or translucent typed planes; with or without a capture cue (drone frustum, survey tick); all built to work in one colour at 16 px. It then screens fresh names with the same layered, fused meaning. The round-1 page is unchanged.

Quick screen, not legal clearance. Collisions were checked with web searches on 3 Oct 2026. No trademark registers were searched and no domains were verified.

Recommendation

Stratlas, with R5 "Plumb through layers"

  • The mark says the product in one figure. Several georeferenced layers, one shared coordinate, measured from above. The plumb line, station ring and bob tie it to survey and capture without a cliché drone silhouette, and it is the direct descendant of the mark the founder picked.
  • It survives being small. The plates are separated by real cut gaps, so the mark reads in one colour, at 16 px and on any ground. Three layers keep each band at least one pixel thick at 16 px.
  • The name explains itself. Strata + atlas lands on first read with English-speaking engineers and is easy to say in Arabic. The founder already likes it.
  • The one risk to clear first: the Strat- stem is crowded in drone software (Trimble Stratus above all). Put Stratlas vs Stratus in front of a trademark attorney before spending on it. If it fails, Atlastra keeps the same meaning and the same mark with a cleaner screen. Nadeed is the strongest Arabic-first alternative.
  • Runner-up mark: R2 (frustum), for a more explicit capture story on large formats. It can be the illustrative companion to R5.

1 · Six refinements of the layered mark

All six sit on a 64-unit grid with a 2:1 isometric slope. Plate vertices fall on whole units, and every gap is real transparency (masked), not a painted background, so each mark works in one colour on any ground. Each card shows: the symbol on dark and light; a one-colour test at 48 and 16 px; the horizontal lockup; and the app icon at 512 (shown at 112), 32 and 16 px.

R1 · Three plates

3 layersisometric 2:1mono-first

The base construction. Three plates on a 2:1 isometric grid (every vertex on a 4-unit grid, gaps cut as real transparency). It is the most compact and reads cleanly at 16 px. On its own it sits close to the generic "layers" UI icon, which is why the other refinements add capture cues.

R2 · Four plates + frustum

4 layersisometricdrone frustum

Four layers (map, cloud, mesh, video). A drone pose sits above, off-centre, and its view frustum lands exactly on the top plate's corners, so the top layer becomes the camera footprint. This is capture plus fusion in one figure.

R3 · Five contour bands

5 layersflat bandssurvey benchmark

Flat bands, like strata in section or contours in profile. They settle from wavy terrain at the top to a flat datum at the bottom. A trig-point triangle marks the survey benchmark. Calm and cartographic, but it loses the 3D read.

Files: logos/round2/stratlas-r3-contours5-symbol.svg -mark.svg -icon.svg

R4 · Fused typed planes

4 layerstranslucentmap + cloud + mesh + video

Four translucent planes, each showing its data type: map grid, point cloud, mesh, and a video frame on top. It is the most literal "fusion" story and good as a hero illustration. The icon has to drop the textures, so it is not an icon-grade master.

Files: logos/round2/stratlas-r4-fused4-symbol.svg -mark.svg -icon.svg

R5 · Plumb through layers

3 layersisometricplumb bob

Refines the round-1 mark the founder liked. The stack is threaded on one plumb line: a survey-station ring above, a plumb bob below pointing at the ground mark. Every layer is tied to a single coordinate. An earlier version cut the line through the plates, but that split them into leaf shapes, so the line is implied through the stack instead. It is the clearest "one point, every layer" statement.

Files: logos/round2/stratlas-r5-plumb3-symbol.svg -mark.svg -icon.svg

R6 · Five offset strata

5 layersoffset parallelogramsdip

Five thin plates, each stepped 3 units along the slope, like dipping strata in an outcrop. It has the most geological character and the most motion. At 16 px the fifth layer fuses, so 5 is the ceiling for layer count.

2 · Names that carry the layered meaning

About 24 fresh names were screened, built from strata, layer, stack, atlas, horizon and survey roots across Latin, Greek, Arabic and coined compounds. Five are shortlisted, Stratlas included. Each is set in the R5 lockup so they compare like for like.

Stratlas ستراتلاس

Recommended

Coined compound: strata + atlas

Meaning
An atlas made of layers. Every dataset is one stratum in a single georeferenced stack. Its meaning is obvious in English on first read.
Say it
STRAT-las. Arabic: ستراتلاس. Some Arabic speakers add a lead vowel (is-trat-las), which is harmless.
Screen
Caution The exact name is held only by an archived open-source hobby tool that renders maps from 1990s strategy games (alpha 2012, repo archived 2021). The real risk is the crowded Strat- stem in drone software: Trimble Stratus (drone data platform), open-source Stratum GCS and STRATA (UAV video-to-3D), and the StratOS drone simulator. Stratlas vs Stratus needs an attorney's view first.
Domains
stratlas.com is not verified; it may be parked. stratlas.ai looks likely to be free.

Atlastra أتلاسترا

Coined compound: atlas + strata (and Latin astra, stars)

Meaning
The same atlas-of-layers idea, read from the other end. The hidden astra nods to surveying by the sky (GNSS, the drone overhead).
Say it
at-LAS-tra. Arabic: أتلاسترا. No p or v; easy in both languages. Some readers may split it as at-last-ra.
Screen
Clear on quick screen The only exact use is a small electronic-music artist. The UAE's Astra Tech (Botim) shares the "astra" ending but is in a different field. No software, GIS, drone or inspection product was found.
Domains
A rare coinage, so the .com and .ai look likely to be free (not verified).

Nadeed نَضِيد

Arabic-rooted, classical word

Meaning
Classical Arabic for "set in layers, stacked tier on tier, neatly arranged". It is the strata idea in one native word, and it is built on ḍād, the letter Arabic is named for (lughat al-ḍād).
Say it
na-DEED. Arabic: نَضِيد. English speakers say d for ض, which is fine.
Screen
Clear on quick screen No product or company found in English or Arabic searches. The nearest names are the Nadeem chat app and NADSOFT (web agencies). The word has a classical and Qur'anic register (50:10), which is dignified but should get a native-speaker check. Its meaning is opaque outside Arabic.
Domains
The .com and .ai look likely to be free (not verified). nadeed.ae and .com.kw are worth securing.

Tibaq طِبَاق

Arabic-rooted, classical word

Meaning
"Layers one above another", as in the classical image of the heavens in layers. In rhetoric it also means antithesis. Short and strong.
Say it
ti-BAAQ (q as in Qatar). Arabic: طِبَاق.
Screen
Caution It is a near-homophone of Tabaq, a UAE food-delivery app (ae.tabaq) and another Tabaq meals app, which is a sound collision in the core market. A Malayalam web magazine uses Tibaq. No software, GIS or drone use was found.
Domains
tibaq.com is likely taken or parked (the magazine is on tibaq.in). Plan on tibaq.ai or a prefix.

Strataxis ستراتاكسيس

Coined compound: strata + axis

Meaning
Layers registered on one shared axis, which is the plumb line in mark R5.
Say it
strat-AX-is. Arabic: ستراتاكسيس. Long, with a str- cluster.
Screen
Caution Several management consultancies (Switzerland, India, the Netherlands, Africa) use it to mean "strategy + axis", and most readers will take it that way too. No software, GIS or drone use was found.
Domains
strataxis.com is listed for sale on a name marketplace, so it can be bought.

Method and next steps

Mark construction

  • The grid is 64 units with a 2:1 isometric slope (26.57°), so plate edges render as clean pixel steps.
  • Gaps are 2 to 3 units, cut with SVG masks. Lines are 2.6 to 4 units. Accent is always the top or capture layer.
  • Icons use the same geometry scaled about 6.4 times on a 512 rounded square. R4 drops its data textures in the icon.
  • Wordmarks are live IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Arabic text. Outline and kern them before production.

Before committing

  1. Get an attorney's opinion on Stratlas vs Trimble Stratus, then a full search in classes 9 and 42 (WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO, UAE, Kuwait, KSA, GCC), including Arabic script.
  2. Check and buy domains: .com, .ai, .ae, .com.kw.
  3. Have a native speaker review Nadeed and Tibaq for register and dialect readings.
  4. Test R5 at 16 px in the real Windows tray and macOS dock before locking it.
