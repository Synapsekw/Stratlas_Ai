Synapse Solutions · Naming exploration · 3 Oct 2026

Name and Logo Options

Name candidates for the offline-first desktop workspace. It fuses drone video and flight logs, 3D models, point clouds, maps and photos of industrial assets into one scene and one timeline, with an agent in every window. Buyers are asset-integrity managers in oil and gas, utilities and construction, and government clients in Kuwait and the UAE.

The bar for a name: credible in a ministry meeting, easy to say in English and Arabic, able to stand on its own, and free of software, GIS, drone and inspection conflicts.

Quick screen, not legal clearance. Conflicts were checked with web searches on 3 Oct 2026. No trademark registers were searched and no domain availability was verified. See the method notes before committing.

Recommendation

Muqantar, with the almucantar-rings mark

  • It comes from the region's own science. Al-muqanṭarāt are the astrolabe's circles of equal altitude, and English astronomy still uses the word as almucantar. A Kuwaiti ministry and a European EPC contractor can both take it seriously.
  • It means what the product does. Altitude, contour and vantage point. The mark uses the real geometry of an astrolabe plate, so the story is true and not decoration.
  • Easy to say in both languages. In English it is moo-KAN-tar, with the q said as in "Qatar". In Arabic it is مُقَنْطَر. There are no p or v sounds.
  • Cleanest screen of the four. No software, GIS, drone or inspection use was found. The rare word should make the .com and .ai easy to get.
  • Cost: nobody knows the word yet. It needs a one-line story on the splash screen and in the deck ("named after the astrolabe's circles of altitude").

Runner-up: Ocellus, if you want an international, Latin tone. If sales would rather lean on the parent brand, the fallback is Synapse Atlas: quickest to launch, hardest to protect.

Shortlist

Four names, one per style. Each has two logo concepts drawn from the subject itself: layers, the drone pose frustum, survey bearings, contour lines and lenses. Every concept is shown on a dark and a light ground, with its app icon at the 512 px master size and at 32 and 16 px.

1

Muqantar مُقَنْطَر

Recommended

Arabic-rooted, heritage astronomy term

Meaning
The astrolabe's circles of equal altitude, al-muqanṭarāt, which entered English as almucantar. The word itself means arched or vaulted. It fits a tool that shows one asset from every altitude at once.
Say it
moo-KAN-tar (the q is said like the k in Qatar). Arabic: مُقَنْطَر, muqanṭar.
Screen
Clear on quick screen No software, GIS, drone, reality-capture or inspection product found under Muqantar, Mukantar or Mucantar. The only near-matches are two small consultancies called Almucantar (UK IT consultancy, inc. 2009; Paris management consultancy). Because the word is rare, the .com and .ai look likely to be free. That is not verified.

A. Almucantar rings

Circles of equal altitude crowding toward the zenith, drawn the way they sit on an astrolabe plate. They also read as terrain contours with a drone point at the summit.

Ocellus أوسيلوس

Latin real word (zoology)

Meaning
"Little eye": the simple eyes of bees (drones included) and dragonflies, which hold the horizon and sense attitude. That is the same job as a pose estimate.
Say it
oh-SEL-us. Arabic: أوسيلوس. English stress can drift to OSS-el-us, so lead with audio in demos.
Screen
Caution Ocellus Inc. is a cloud and IT consultancy (Schaumburg, IL), and another Ocellus Inc. makes aerogel materials (Livermore, CA). No drone, GIS or imaging product was found. The .com is almost certainly taken, so plan on ocellus.ai, ocellus.app or a prefix such as getocellus.

A. Lens axis

A biconvex lens in section with two rays meeting at the focal point. This is the camera model behind every frame projected onto the asset.

B. Ocelli triad

The three simple eyes over a horizon line. Three points fix a plane and a pose, which is the basis of triangulation.

Stratlas ستراتلاس

Compound: strata + atlas

Meaning
An atlas made of layers. Every dataset (mesh, cloud, map, video) is one stratum in a single georeferenced stack.
Say it
STRAT-las. Arabic: ستراتلاس. Some Arabic speakers add a lead vowel (is-trat-las), which is normal and harmless.
Screen
Caution, low The only use found is an open-source hobby tool (GPL, SourceForge and GitHub) that renders maps from real-time strategy games. Its repository was archived in 2021, and it is not commercial. No GIS, drone or inspection product was found. The name is coined and self-explanatory in English.

A. Plumb through layers

Three georeferenced layers (model, point cloud, map) held together by one plumb line through a single coordinate.

Synapse Atlas سينابس أطلس

Synapse family: house mark + descriptor

Meaning
The company's atlas of every asset it has flown. It sells under the parent's name, which already has standing with GCC clients.
Say it
SIN-aps AT-las. Arabic: سينابس أطلس.
Screen
Caution Both words are crowded in software. "Synapse" is used by Microsoft Azure Synapse Analytics and by Atlas Technology's Synapse wireless monitoring system (UK). "Atlas" is used by MongoDB Atlas and several drone makers. No product called "Synapse Atlas" was found. It is the weakest of the four to protect on its own and depends on the house mark. No new domain is needed (synapse-solutions.ai/atlas).

Method and next steps

How the screen was run

  • About 45 names were generated across five styles: coined, real word, compound, Arabic-rooted and Latin-rooted. Names with p or v sounds were avoided because Arabic has neither.
  • Each name was searched against software, GIS, drone, reality-capture, inspection and GCC technology companies. Names whose conflict is common industry knowledge were noted without a fresh search.
  • Dropped for a clear conflict in an adjacent market, or for a confusingly similar sound (Cardo and CARTO, Rasida and Rasid).
  • Caution means the name is used by companies in unrelated fields or by inactive projects.

Before committing

  1. Run a full trademark search in classes 9 and 42: WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO, EUIPO, plus the UAE, Kuwait, KSA and GCC registers, including Arabic-script forms.
  2. Check and register domains: .com, .ai, .ae and .com.kw.
  3. Test pronunciation with five Arabic and five English speakers who have only seen the name written.
  4. Have a native speaker review the Arabic spellings and any dialect readings.
  5. The wordmarks are live IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Arabic text. Have a type designer outline and kern them before production.
