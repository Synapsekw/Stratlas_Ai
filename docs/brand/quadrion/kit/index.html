Quadrion AI

Mark: Quarter: three quarters of a ring and a mint square. Three dimensions of space and a fourth that is different. It reads as a Q. Type: Archivo Expanded Bold, outlined. Colour: Mission, the palette the app already uses. Tagline: Four dimensions. One view.

Lockups

Use the horizontal lockup by default and the stacked one where space is square. Clear space on every side equals the height of the capital letters. "AI" may be dropped where the product name is already clear (title bar, splash footers).

Rules

Keep the gaps open

The gaps between the quarters and the square are real transparency. Never fill them in or place the mark on a busy photo without a solid ground behind it.

One mint square

Only the square takes the accent. In one-colour use all four pieces are the same colour.

Minimum size

Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Mark alone: 16 px, using the small-size cut.
