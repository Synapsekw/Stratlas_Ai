Rules

Keep the plates apart

The cuts between plates are real transparency. Never fill them in or place the mark on a busy photo without a solid plate behind it.

One lit plate

Only the top plate takes the accent. In one-colour use all four plates are the same colour.

Minimum size

Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Mark alone: 16 px, using the small-size cut.
