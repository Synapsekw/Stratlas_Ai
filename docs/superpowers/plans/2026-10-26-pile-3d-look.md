# Pile 3D look (plan, 26 Oct 2026)

Goal: an opened stockpile in 3D looks like the approved spike look A (a lifted "glass box" prism),
with a height-above-base option, an Edit boundary action on the pile's menu and an oblique fly on
open. Replaces the throwaway spike at `40e968793`.

## Decided

Founder:

1. Opened pile in 3D = look A: the pile surface lifted above the ground in solid red (about 0.9
   opacity, hillshaded so the relief reads), no hatch; a translucent blue base clipped to the
   boundary, on the base surface; a white glass box frame (the boundary at the top and at the
   base, a thin white post at each vertex); the terrain around it untouched.
2. Height above base colouring (blue, cyan, green, yellow, red) with a small "Height above base
   0 to X m" legend in the 3D view, as a pile panel option "Surface: Solid | Height".
3. "Edit boundary" on the pile's right-click menu (3D, map, sidebar: the action registry) enters
   the existing `startEditing()` vertex mode; the volume goes stale and recomputes as today.
4. Opening a pile flies the camera to an oblique view (about 38 degrees) that frames the whole
   pile.

Orchestrator's calls (kept unless the code says otherwise):

- a. Look A applies to the opened pile only; other piles keep outline, label and the light draped
  fill. A kit pile that has a volume measurement no longer draws the kit's own body, plate or
  hatch (the measurement's look stands for it, as its outline already did).
- b. Opening a pile no longer draws its own cut and fill heat map in 3D; Show, Cut & fill still
  draws the site's. In change mode (Show, Cut & fill on) the prism's top takes the site cut and
  fill colours instead of solid red; the frame and base stay.
- c. Solid or Height is a session view preference (`pileView`), not saved on the measurement.
- d. Fly on open from the sidebar, the inventory, the map and the next and previous pile buttons;
  a click on the pile in 3D flies only when the pile is not already mostly in view.
- e. Right-click and click hit-testing in 3D also take the lifted top and the box's sides.
- f. While the boundary is edited the prism hides and the draped outline with handles shows; the
  prism comes back on finish or cancel.

My call: the look lives in a new renderer module (`survey/pile/pileLook*.ts`), not in
`@aio/volumetric`'s scene controller. The controller draws the stockpile kit's piles from its
worker; a survey project's piles are measurements the kit never sees, so putting the look there
would mean feeding measurements into the kit store. The controller only learns to skip the body of
a measured kit pile (call a).
