# Inspect Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** One screen, Inspect, replaces Media and Detections: the project's photos on the left, the real `PhotoViewer` in the centre with your findings drawn solid and the AI's suggestions dashed orange, one "Findings on this photo" list on the right, one Ask AI button with a scope, and a review queue for working through AI results.

**Architecture:** Inspect is a new screen (`screens/Inspect.tsx`) composed from focused parts in a new `renderer/inspect/` folder: a pure findings model over the issues and the review's detections, accept and dismiss actions over the existing detections store, a headless Ask AI engine that resolves the backend from a Settings default and starts today's cloud, local model or local detector run code, and the photo column, findings panel, review queue and viewer. `PhotoViewer` gains optional props to draw extra dashed shapes, so the Lightbox is untouched. Media and Detections leave the sidebar, the router, the palette and the agent's screen list (`media` and `detections` stay as agent aliases that open Inspect), and their code is deleted once their unit and e2e coverage has moved to Inspect.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, React 19, zustand, zod 4, Vitest (jsdom), Playwright with Electron.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-inspect-design.md` (founder-approved 24 Oct; its "Settled after the builder check" and "Open points" win over its earlier text). ## The run paths Ask AI reuses

- **Cloud vision:** `AiDetectDialog.tsx:175-241` builds a run id, `aiPassName`, `noteAssessed`, then `startDetectRun` (`detections/runner.ts:73`) with deps `ai:detect`, `ai:cancel`, `prepareItem`, `toDraftDetections` and `dispatch({ type: 'add' })`. Results land per batch, so suggestions stream in. Provider, cost and preview: `AiDetectDialog.tsx:154-162, 273-309` (`estimateDetect`, `PROVIDER_LABELS`, `modelLabel`) and its preview section.
- **Local model (ONNX):** `LocalDetect.tsx:299-345`: `inference:run` with model, items, class map, `minConfidence`, optional `tile`; progress events `inference:progress`; the pass is written by main at the end, then `loadDetections(projectId, true)`. Suggestions appear when the run ends; the strip counts photos as they finish.
- **Local detectors (VLM):** `DetectorRunDialog.tsx:195-250`: `planRuns` and `runRequest` (`detectors/runPlan.ts`), `jobs.getState().start(...)`, queued runs via `detect:queue`; `jobs.ts:227` (`PASS_WRITERS`) reloads the review when `detect.vlm` finishes. Suggestions appear per finished job.

Spec interpretation (no founder question needed): "suggestions stream in as each photo finishes" holds for cloud runs; local model and detector runs write their pass when they finish, so their suggestions arrive at the end while the strip counts done photos live. Video and 360 walk frames keep today's reach as a fourth Ask AI scope, "Frames…" (clips: cloud or local; walks: local only; detectors: photos only), so `detect-walk.spec.ts` keeps its coverage.

## How to test (for the founder)

1. Open a project with photos. The sidebar shows **Inspect** where Media and Detections were.
2. Click a photo on the left. Press **B**, drag a box on the photo, pick a class and severity, **Create issue**. The tile shows a coloured count; the finding is in the list on the right and in Issues.
3. Click **Ask AI** (This photo). With a local model or detector installed it starts at once; with only cloud AI allowed a line asks "Send 1 photo to …, about …?" with **Send**. Dashed orange boxes appear with a confidence; the tile gets an orange count.
4. Press **A** on a suggestion: it becomes yours (solid, severity colour) with your last class and severity. Press **D** on another: it goes away.
5. Pick **All (n)** and Ask AI. The strip at the top counts photos as they finish; **Stop** works.
6. Click **To review**, then **Review n suggestions**: the queue shows "1 of n", a crop, **Accept (A)**, **Fix (F)**, **Not a fault (D)**, the slider and "Accept all … above n%". **Esc** returns to the photos.
7. In Settings, AI, "AI for Ask AI": switch to Local only or Cloud; the local model's classes are mapped there.
8. In Issues, open a finding's photo ("Open in Inspect" in the lightbox): Inspect opens on that photo with the finding selected.
9. Right-click a photo tile: place, move, direction, as 360, Edit position and Analyse thermal are all still there.
