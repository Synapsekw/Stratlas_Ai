# Pinned media

## What changed

- Landed on main as `7bf27fd2` (PR #166, CI 16/16 green plus 1 skipped). Design `plans/2026-10-17-pinned-media-design.md`, plan `plans/2026-10-17-pinned-media.md`.
- Bug fix: a ground-level Insta360 360 (2:1 JPEG, no GPS, no GPano XMP) imported as a photo, because a ready-made 360 without lat/lon was skipped (`PanoRef.pos` was required). It now imports as an unplaced 360 ("Not placed"), also on a local-grid project. Insta360 `.insp` stills import (dual-fisheye ones are skipped with a reason, night equirects are not misread). Treat as 360 / Treat as photo fixes already-imported items in place.
- Feature: Place on site pins a photo or 360 on the 3D scene, map or orthophoto (eye height 1.6 m). A 360 is lined up by clicking the same landmark in the 360 and the scene (two pairs averaged, pairs more than 45 deg apart rejected), then fine-tuned by dragging the arrow. Move pin, Set direction, Remove pin (restores the GPS place when there was one) are on right-click and in Media. Pins follow the date line-up shift; pinned photos ignore old hand alignment.
- Founder addition: every placed photo and 360 on the map is a clickable marker plate with a hover preview (the old 4.5 px black dots missed clicks and lost them to issues on top).
- Contract: `PanoRef.pos` optional, additive `MediaPin` on `PhotoRef`/`PanoRef`, `LayerPatch.mediaPin`, `builder:convertMedia`. Builds 0.19 and earlier refuse a project with an unplaced 360.
- Fixed on the way: menus and pin mounts read the active 3D view or map once and missed it when it came up at the same time (`useSyncExternalStore` hooks in `builder/activeViews.ts`); map plates drew unshifted on lined-up dates; a pinned 360's arrow stole clicks meant for its marker.

## Why

A ground 360 with no GPS had no way into the project as a 360, and photos without GPS had no place at all. Built while the UI rework and Line up dates landed; main was merged in (not rebased) and the branch landed in the UI session's merge window, returning the lock.

## How to test

1. Open a project with a ground 360 that imported as a photo, go to Media, right-click it, Treat as 360: it moves to panoramas with "Not placed".
2. Right-click it, Place on site: a strip appears bottom-centre; click a spot on the point cloud or a model; a pin appears and the 360 opens beside the scene.
3. Click a landmark in the 360, then the same landmark in 3D: the 360 turns to face it; a second pair refines; Done.
4. Click the pin's marker: the 360 opens facing the landmark; drag the pin's arrow to adjust.
5. On the map, photos and 360s are markers with a hover preview; clicking a photo opens it beside the map.
6. Right-click the pin, Remove pin: back to "Not placed".
7. Drop an Insta360 `.insp` or a 2:1 JPEG with no GPS: it is offered as a 360 and imports not placed.
