# Line up dates

## What changed

- A survey date can be lined up with another: `Capture.lineUp` (metres east and north, absolute, at most 500 m) is applied at read time by every view and job (engine adapters, point clouds, maps, globe, annotations, measurements, issues and reports, video, photos, extents, AI tools, the Python readers). Common layers, basemaps, terrain and change results never move; files and stored coordinates are never rewritten. Data conventions section 38 and a contract row.
- New `change.lineup` pipeline job measures the gap (coarse to fine phase correlation on the orthos, DSM slopes as fallback) and refuses with a plain reason when it cannot trust the result.
- Two ways in: a notice when a new survey does not line up with another, and Line up dates in the Changes panel where imagery or surface change refuses. The step picks the date to keep, shows shift, confidence, what moves and a Before/After swipe; Undo in the notice, Remove line-up in the date folder menu.
- Rebased onto the island layout, the line-up UI moved onto the shared Button and tokens, landed as #164 (`348b92d6`) under the merge lock lent by the UI rework session; the lock went back to it.

## Why

Two flights without RTK sit metres apart, so imagery change refused ("2.01 m apart; the largest shift allowed is 2 px") and swipe, pins and 3D disagreed between dates. The founder chose one correction per date that every consumer honours over a fix inside the comparison only.

## How to test

1. Open a project with two survey dates whose orthos sit a couple of metres apart.
2. Wait for "... doesn't line up with ...", click Line up dates, choose the date to keep, check the shift, swipe Before and After, click Line up and read the notice.
3. Compare dates: swipe the two orthos; look at a pin on both dates and in the 3D view.
4. Changes panel: run imagery change; it runs instead of refusing.
5. Undo from the notice; then line up again and remove it from the date folder's right-click menu.
