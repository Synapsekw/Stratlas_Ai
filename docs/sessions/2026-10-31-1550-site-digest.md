# Site digest, volume trend and QA fitness flags

## What changed

- This PR adds the site digest (intelligence review I2, study items 5.2, 5.4, 5.5 and 5.8). A new package, `@aio/insights` (schema only), holds `siteInsights(snapshot)`: pure, rules-based, no AI, never writes. Each insight has a severity, a short text (an `insights.*` key with its values), evidence (measurements, dates, QA results, change sets) and suggested actions. Nine rules, one fixture test each: survey on hold, checkpoint RMSE over the limit, latest date not checked or without a verdict, latest date passed without checkpoints, change set out of date after a line-up, change items to review on the latest pair, a figure that breaks from its trend, a figure that moved over 20 % and 5 m³, a falling figure that runs out within 60 days.
- Volume rate and forecast: `volumeTrend` gives the least-squares rate in m³ a day over the last five dates, days to empty, the figure in 30 days and a robust score of the last rate. With fewer than three dates it says `not-enough-dates` (two dates still give the plain rate, never a forecast). Its Python twin is `aio_pipelines/survey/trend.py`; both reproduce the shared fixture `packages/schema/src/__fixtures__/survey/trend.json`.
- `ComparisonResult.qaFlags` (contract row): the app stamps each result and series row with the QA fitness flags of its date (`qa-hold`, `qa-released`, `qa-unchecked`, `no-checkpoints`, `rmse-over-limit`) as derived data, once the project's QA files are read. The earliest date is never flagged unchecked.
- The digest sits at the top of the Project tab (one mount line in `MeasureBench`), in its own folder `renderer/insights/` with its own CSS and the new `insights` i18n area. It follows the QA, measurement and change stores, so when the checks after a new date (I1) land their verdict and change set, it updates without a click. A click on a line shows its evidence; its buttons run the right-click registry's item (Fly to, Compare dates) or open Survey QA on the date or Changes on the pair. It shows only on projects with two or more survey dates and folds to four lines.
- E2e: `site-digest.spec.ts` on the change demo.

## Why

The numbers existed but nothing told a person what mattered after a new date. The digest turns I1's QA verdicts and change sets, and the volume history, into a short list with the action that answers each line.

## How to test

1. Open the change demo (Demo change site, 2 dates). The Project tab starts with SITE DIGEST: "Survey of (later date) has not been checked yet", with Open survey QA.
2. Click Open survey QA: the Survey QA tab opens on that date.
3. Open the Changes tab, pick the two dates and run the comparison. Back on the Project tab, the digest now also says "N changes from (earlier) to (later) to review", without reopening anything.
4. Click Review changes: the Changes tab opens on that pair. Click the line itself: the same.
5. On a stockpile project with three or more dates (the quarry demo once its surfaces and volumes are worked out), piles that moved over 20 % since the date before, or that run out within 60 days at their rate, have a line with Fly to and Compare dates.
6. Hold a date in Survey QA (a failed check at level Moderate or Strict): the digest puts "Survey of (date) is on hold after a failed QA check" first, with how many figures were measured on it.
7. A project with one survey date shows no digest.
