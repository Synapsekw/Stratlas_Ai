# Site digest, volume trend and QA fitness flags

## What changed

- This PR adds the site digest (intelligence review I2, study items 5.2, 5.4, 5.5 and 5.8). A new package, `@aio/insights` (schema only), holds `siteInsights(snapshot)`: pure, rules-based, no AI, never writes. Each insight has a severity, a short text (an `insights.*` key with its values; quantities in SI for the reader to format), evidence (measurements, dates, QA results, change sets) and suggested actions. Ten rules, one fixture test each: the latest held date (critical) and earlier dates still on hold (one note), checkpoint RMSE over the limit, latest date not checked or without a verdict, latest date passed without checkpoints, change set out of date after a line-up, change items to review on the latest pair, a figure that breaks from its trend, a figure that moved over 20 % and 5 m³, and a falling figure that at its rate is empty within 60 days of today (14: a warning) or would already be empty (a warning).
- The figures are the inventory's counted figures per date (`inventorySeries` with the app's own staleness checks, through `useInventory`), the volume card's rule: volume tools only, the first comparison, stale and refused figures left out, a pile fixed to its dates keeping its stored figure only. So the digest's numbers are the card's. Quantities show in the site's units and number style, through the formatters the cards use.
- Volume rate and forecast: `volumeTrend` gives the least-squares rate per day over the last five dates, the date it empties, the figure in 30 days and a robust score of the last rate. With fewer than three dates it says `not-enough-dates` (two dates still give the plain rate, never a forecast). A date that is not an ISO date and a value that is not a number are left out. Its Python twin is `aio_pipelines/survey/trend.py`; both reproduce the shared fixture `packages/schema/src/__fixtures__/survey/trend.json` (11 cases).
- QA fitness flags (`qa-hold`, `qa-released`, `qa-unchecked`, `no-checkpoints`, `rmse-over-limit`): `qaFlagsFor` and `resultQaFlags` in `@aio/insights` work them out when read, for the digest now and for the report and the agent later. They are never stored on a result, so opening a project leaves its measurements saved.
- The digest sits at the top of the Project tab (one mount line in `MeasureBench`), in its own folder `renderer/insights/` with its own CSS and the new `insights` i18n area. It follows the QA, measurement, inventory and change stores, so when the checks after a new date (I1) land their verdict and change set, it updates without a click. A click on a line shows its evidence (or says it is gone); its buttons run the right-click registry's item (Fly to, Compare dates, with the menu's own refusals) or open Survey QA on the date or Changes on the pair. It shows only on projects with two or more survey dates, once their QA files are read, and folds to four lines.
- No contract change: flags are derived at read time. `QaState.loadedFor` is app state only.
- E2e: `site-digest.spec.ts` on the change demo.

## Why

The numbers existed but nothing told a person what mattered after a new date. The digest turns I1's QA verdicts and change sets, and the volume history, into a short list with the action that answers each line.

## How to test

1. Open the change demo (Demo change site, 2 dates). The Project tab starts with SITE DIGEST: "Survey of (later date) has not been checked yet", with Open survey QA. Under TOOLS, Save stays off and reads All saved.
2. Click Open survey QA: the Survey QA tab opens on that date.
3. Open the Changes tab, pick the two dates and run the comparison. Back on the Project tab, the digest now also says "N changes from (earlier) to (later) to review", without reopening anything.
4. Click Review changes, or the line itself: the Changes tab opens on that pair. Click Compare dates: the two dates show side by side, as from the date's right-click menu.
5. On a stockpile project with three or more dates and volumes worked out (the quarry demo once its surfaces are prepared), a pile that moved over 20 % since the date before reads "(pile) grew (volume in the site units) (30%) since (date)", the same figure as its volume card; a pile drawn down reads "At this rate, (pile) is empty by (date)".
6. Hold two dates in Survey QA (failed checks at level Moderate or Strict): the later one reads "Survey of (date) is on hold after a failed QA check", first and marked as needing action; the earlier one is a note "1 earlier date is still on hold".
7. A project with one survey date shows no digest.
