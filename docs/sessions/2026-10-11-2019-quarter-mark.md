# The Quarter mark replaces the Time steps icon

## What changed

- The mark is now the Quarter: three quarters of a ring and a mint square, a Q. One geometry module (`packages/brand/src/quarter.ts`) feeds the brand kit, the app icon, the app symbol and the motion.
- Brand kit, app, installer and Store images regenerated (`kit.mjs`, then `pnpm icons`). From 32 px down the ring is drawn in one piece.
- `BrandMotion` plays three motions: Step out (launch screen intro), Quarter turns (loader on a project card and while the globe loads) and the full sequence (Settings, About). Reduced motion stops all of it.
- The launch screen keeps its pointer response: the four pieces lean toward the pointer, the mint square most.
- Branch `feat/quarter-mark` (11 commits) handed to the Orchestrator. `pnpm check` green; `launch-gate` and `smoke` specs pass the way CI runs them, also at device scale 1. `dist:win` was not run.

## Why

The founder was not happy with the icon and asked for options with motion, where a set of pieces forms several shapes and settles into the logo. Two boards of options led to the Quarter, cut A1, with Step out, Quarter turns and the full sequence. The board is kept at `docs/brand/quadrion/quarter/index.html`.

## How to test

1. Install the build and start it. The taskbar, window and title bar show the new mark. A pinned shortcut can keep the old picture until Windows refreshes its icon cache.
2. On the launch screen the mark comes up as a closed ring and its fourth piece steps out and turns into the mint square. Move the mouse: the pieces lean toward it, the square most.
3. Press Esc during the intro on the next start: the mark stands complete at once.
4. **Projects**: open a project. Its card shows the mark turning a quarter at a time beside "Opening". Try it in the light theme too.
5. **Settings**, **About**: the mark forms a map, a model, a row of dates and a globe, then settles.
6. **Settings**, **Appearance**, reduced motion on: none of the above moves.
