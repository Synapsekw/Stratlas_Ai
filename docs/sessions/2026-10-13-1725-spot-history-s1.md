# Spot history S1: one spot on every survey date

## What changed

- Click an image and see that spot on all survey dates: drone photos, ground photos, 360s, walk frames and videos that pass nearby. Pure spot search in `apps/desktop/src/renderer/workspace/spot/` (rules in `spotRules.ts`: ground photo 15 m and 45 deg, 360 25 m, walk 15 m, aerial above 10 m over the target, video sampled every 1 s, flat ground).
- `SpotViewer` modal (mounted in `App.tsx`) with a date strip and Compare (Side by side, Swipe, linked zoom and pan); walk frames open turned to the spot. Entry points: "Other dates" in Media (plus an inline strip), split photo pane, Selection card, Frames pane, walk player.
- Guide section in `docs/guide/04-compare-dates.md`, a `TESTING.md` stage, e2e `apps/desktop/e2e/spot-history.spec.ts`. `.prettierignore` now ignores `.superpowers`.
- Built subagent-driven from the spec and plan (14 tasks), one review per task and a final whole-branch review (7 fixes). No contract change; nothing is written to disk.

## Why

Progress monitoring needs the same place on every date without hunting through each dataset. Founder choices from the clickable designs: A date strip as base, B two dates via Compare, D spot picked on the map later (S2), C spot sheet left to T4. All kinds take part; stepping dates in the viewer never changes the main view's date. Approved deviations: Compare opens inside the viewer; linked zoom for photos and video frames too; empty-slot radius follows the clicked kind ("Not seen" for aerial).

## How to test

1. Open a project with two or more survey dates (the change demo works), go to Media and open a photo: a date strip sits under it and an "Other dates" button is in its header.
2. Other dates: left/right step dates, up/down other images of the same date, hatched slots where nothing is near. Close it: the date bar is unchanged.
3. Compare...: wheel-zoom one side and the other follows; try Swipe and drag the divider. Esc leaves Compare (shows the left date), Esc again closes.
4. 360: select a panorama, Other dates from the Selection card; other dates face the same way.
5. Video: Frames pane, Other dates on a video frame; other dates' clips open at the closest moment.
6. Walk: a placed walk, Other dates in the walk player (manual only, no placed-walk fixture).
