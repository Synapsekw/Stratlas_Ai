# Closed 3D views no longer leak through a video rig

## What changed

- `3e80910af` fixes the renderer leak behind the nightly soak failure, which had been red every night since 19 Oct. The drone menu's footprint effect called `videoRig(scene)`, which created a VideoRig for every 3D view, including in projects with no video. Each rig subscribed to the workspace store and was never disposed, so every closed Stage, its WebGLRenderer, DOM and maps stayed alive.
- The cause is 5491c7cd4 (18 Oct). It moved the drone menu to the `useScene` hook, which, unlike the old hook, does see the 3D view.
- The new `setFrustumOn(handle, on)` remembers the footprint switch per scene and applies it only to a rig that exists. The new `cameraModeOf(handle)` reads the camera mode without creating a rig. The cut-away effect and the two "Inside view" buttons use it, since they had the same leak in real use.
- A regression test in `packages/video/src/rig.test.ts` checks that a scene without video gets no rig and no frame callback.

## Why

The engineering review ranked this P0, because the soak test is the 1.0 stability gate.

## Evidence

- Before the fix, a heap snapshot after 20 cycles held 21 Stages and 21 WebGLRenderers, retained by store listeners through a VideoRig closure. After the fix it holds 1 of each.
- The soak spec at 80 cycles, run the CI way, passes. Renderer heap median goes from 83.1 MB to 86.0 MB, against a limit of 95.4 MB.

## How to test

1. Open a project without video, such as the tank farm demo, then switch to another project, and do that a few times.
2. Open the performance HUD with Ctrl+Shift+F. GPU memory should stay flat instead of rising with each switch.
3. In a project with video, the drone footprint toggle still shows and hides the camera footprint.

## Open threads

- `videoRig()` still creates a rig on first use. The other callers only run when a clip or the align session is active, but a rig that disposes itself with its Stage would close the trap for good.
