# The stage island becomes a notch

## What changed

- The pill-shaped stage island is now a Mac-style notch flush with the stage top, with curved shoulders (design A of three mock-ups the founder picked).
- At rest it always shows the 3D / Map / Split switch, the date with arrows, the live reading and a chevron. There is no proximity wake.
- It opens on a 150 ms hover on the notch itself (not while the sidebar drawer is open), a click, the chevron or T. Unpinned, it closes 300 ms after the pointer leaves, on Esc or on a press outside.
- Open rows: group tabs, Jobs, Keep open and Close, then the quick row. The top line keeps its exact screen position as it opens.
- Keep open (pin) is remembered per computer (`stratlas.islandPinned`). Pinned, it stays open through outside presses, Esc, leaving, group picks and tiles; Close is hidden and the right-panel toggle stays visible. A press outside closes the calendar back to the tools. New i18n keys `island.pin` and `island.unpin`.
- No contract change, no pipeline pack change.

## Why

The founder found the pill annoying: one click too many to get to things. The view switch and date now sit where they are always reachable.

## How to test

1. Open a project with two dates: the notch at the top centre shows 3D / Map / Split, the date with arrows and a chevron.
2. Click Map, then 3D: one click each, the notch stays closed.
3. Rest the pointer on the notch: it opens downwards (tabs, then the quick row) and the top line does not move; move away and it closes.
4. Open it, click the pin (Keep open): it lights up and a thin accent line runs along the bottom. Click in the scene, press Esc, pick Measure: it stays open showing Measure's quick row while Measure opens on the right.
5. Restart the app: the notch starts open.
6. Click the pin again and move away: it closes.
7. Press T: it opens and closes as before.
