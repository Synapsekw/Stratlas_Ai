# Agent actions and read tools (A2)

## What changed

- This PR gives the in-app agent the right-click menus. `list_actions { target }` answers what a person's menu offers on the project, a survey date, layers, measurements, antennas, photos, 360s or the issue rows (ids, labels, why one is greyed, its picks); `run_action { action, target, pick? }` runs one through the same registry, greyed reasons and picks the menus use. A view action (hide, show, fly to, open, compare) runs at once; an action that changes data or writes a file shows the approval card first (per call: `ToolSpec.riskFor`).
- `AGENT_ACTIONS` (`packages/ai/src/action-tool-specs.ts`) is the coverage table: each of the 103 registered actions is covered with a risk or excluded with a reason (file pickers, name fields, hand work in the view, folding a row, closing the project, emptying the trash, removing a walk placement). `apps/desktop/src/renderer/agent/actionParity.test.ts` fails when a registered action has no entry, when an entry names no action, when a covered action takes no target the agent can name, or when a danger action is covered without approval.
- Targets that fit several layers or dates are refused, with each candidate's id and date; exact ids and "earliest" or "latest" are taken. Approval cards show the resolved target (the layer name and date). A date move is awaited, a refused write fails the step, layers whose name keeps a date are listed, and the step has the Datasets Undo. Deletes and other actions that open a confirm box answer "waiting for you to confirm". Review undo is tied to the review step it made (several reopens are one step) and is refused, with the reason on the step, once a later review came in. Step summaries and cards come from the `agentTool` i18n area.
- Ten read tools: `list_surveys`, `set_survey_date` (also "earliest" and "latest", with undo), `list_measurements`, `get_volume` (net, cut, fill, area, peak, tonnes from the pile's material, stale flagged, as the cards show them), `rank_locations` (findings clustered by position or zone, scored by severity and count, each with a point for `fly_to`), `list_detections`, `review_detections` (accept into draft issues, reject or reopen; approval card, undone through the review's undo), `list_jobs`, `thermal_summary` (hot spots hottest first, readings per layer) and `list_walks`.
- The tool list still fits the project: the date tools need a survey date, and measurements, volumes, detections, thermal and walks are offered only where the app does not know the project lacks them (`AgentFacts` gains five optional flags; contract row added). The compact profile gains three short entries only (`list_surveys`, `get_volume`, `run_action`).
- The full prompt gains a short "Survey data" section. `registeredActions()` is added to the `@aio/ui` registry; no shell file changed.
- Eval (E1): new cases on the tank farm (a two-turn "Hide all layers", "Show them again" conversation through `run_action`, ranking, jobs, and no thermal or walk data), the change demo (dates, the earliest date into view, accepted detections) and the quarry (measurements, volumes). The eval state gains the hidden layers, and each case starts with the layers shown again. The scripted stand-in has keywords for these requests, so they run end to end at no cost.

## Why

The intelligence review (agent.md items 4.4 and 4.5) found that the agent reached none of the right-click actions and had no read tool for survey dates, measurements, volumes, detections, jobs, thermal or walks.

## How to test

1. Open a project with two survey dates and a volume measurement, open the agent and ask "What are the survey dates?": it lists them from `list_surveys`. Ask "Bring the earliest survey into view": the date strip moves to it. Click Undo on the step: the date before comes back (or no date, if none was in view). Move the date strip by hand, then click Undo on a new such step: the step stays done and says the date in view changed since.
2. Ask "What is the volume of the stockpile on each survey?": it answers per date from `get_volume`, with tonnes when the pile has a material.
3. Ask "Hide all layers": the layers hide at once, no approval card. Ask "Show them again": they come back.
4. Ask "File the outline layer under the later date": an approval card names the action, the picked date and the layer with its own date (for example "Outline (4 Sep 2024)"), not the words typed. Approve: the step ends once the layer is filed. Undo on the step files it back as the Datasets note's Undo does.
5. Give two layers the same name, then ask the agent to hide that name: it does nothing and answers with both layers, each with its id and date, and asks which one. Ask for a survey date by a month that two dates share ("the 2024-03 survey"): the same, with both dates listed.
6. Ask it to delete a layer and approve the card: the app's own delete box opens, and the step says it is waiting for you to confirm, not that it is done.
7. Open a package (.aio) and ask to delete a layer: the agent says the action is greyed, with the menu's reason.
8. In a project with detections, ask "Which detections are waiting for review?", then "Reject the two least confident": an approval card ("Reject 2 detections"); after approval they are rejected, and Undo on the step brings both back. Reject another detection by hand on the Detections screen, then click Undo on an agent review step: it stays done and says the detections were reviewed again since.
