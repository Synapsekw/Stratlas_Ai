# An opened pile looks like Propeller's in 3D, with Edit boundary on right-click

## What changed

- An opened pile in 3D: its surface in solid red (hillshaded, no hatch) lifted over a translucent blue base on the pile's own base surface, inside a white glass box (outline at top and base, a post at each vertex); the terrain is untouched. Other piles show outline, label and a light fill only.
- Opening a pile from the sidebar, inventory, map or the next and previous buttons flies to an oblique view (38 degrees) fitted to the view; a 3D click flies only when the pile is mostly out of view.
- The pile panel gains Surface: Solid | Height (height above base, blue to red, legend in the view), a view preference, not saved on the pile.
- Edit boundary on the pile's right-click menu (shared action registry), entering the existing vertex edit; greyed with a reason while drawing, for packages, reviewers, hidden or already edited piles. The box hides while editing.
- Opening a pile no longer turns on its cut and fill overlay in 3D; with Show, Cut & fill on, the box top takes the cut and fill colours. An independent review's five findings are fixed. No contract or pack change.

## Why

The founder found the old translucent red body with hatch and the blue overlay hard to read and asked for Propeller's look; they picked look A of three from an in-app spike (height colouring as an option), and asked for boundary editing from the right-click menu.

## How to test

1. Open the Quarry demo and switch to 3D.
2. In the sidebar under Measurements click Stockpile SP1: the camera flies to it, showing the red pile over a blue base in a white box.
3. In the right panel under Surface click Height: the pile shades blue to red with a legend at the bottom left; click Solid to go back.
4. Right-click the pile in 3D and choose Edit boundary: the box hides and corner handles appear; drag a corner, click Done in the panel; the volume updates and the box returns.
5. Click the next pile arrow at the top of the panel: the camera moves to SP2.
