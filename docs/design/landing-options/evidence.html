THE OBSERVATION STAYS CONNECTED

One finding can have
more than one sighting.

A photo, a video event and a position in 3D can describe the same physical issue. Keep them together so review does not become a hunt through disconnected files.

01

Annotate where you see it

The platform supports points, boxes, rotated boxes and polygons on images, plus annotations on video, models, point clouds and maps. Choose the appropriate class and severity for the inspection.

02

Carry observations between views

With a registered camera pose and scene geometry, image or video observations can be back-projected into the spatial view. Source sightings stay linked to the issue so reviewers can return to the image or moment.

03

Track a video observation over time

Video annotations use keyframes and time ranges. Define when an event starts and ends, and adjust its shape at key moments as the drone moves.

04

Review a register, not a pile of files

Filter by severity, class, status, source or text. Group findings by zone, review statuses, merge related sightings, and use undo or redo while refining the register.

05

Ask with the evidence in view

Open an agent panel bound to a photo, video frame, 3D view or findings context. The platform can capture the current view, find nearby issues, summarize by zone or class, and help with analysis. Cloud requests and write actions stay subject to the configured permissions and approvals.
