Different audiences need different levels of detail. The platform supports a report to read, structured data to reuse, and a project to explore.

01

Inspection and issue-register PDFs

Generate a report with summary charts by severity, class and zone, a findings register, and issue pages with a source photo and a 3D view. Choose report sections, issue-page scope and your report branding in the desktop application.

02

Structured exports

Export a CSV register for analysis, GeoJSON for spatial workflows, or supported annotation formats for downstream use. Exported positions retain their coordinate context; image annotations retain their source image relationship.

03

A portable inspection workspace

Create an .aio package containing chosen layers and the evidence needed to review them. Recipients can explore a read-only project, with optional passphrase protection and package permissions governing export and extraction.

04

Reports alongside the original evidence

Read delivered PDF reports inside the platform. Keep the report accessible alongside models, scans, video and the findings register so the final document remains part of the review workspace.
