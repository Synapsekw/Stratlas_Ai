A close-up gives you the texture. The model gives you the location. The inspection keeps the connection between them.

01

The mask follows the geometry

These are the source inspection's textured surface patches, positioned in the same coordinate frame as the flare stack. Orbit around a flange or along the cladding and the marked regions remain attached to their mapped surfaces.

02

Separate rust from surface oxidation

Switch the class filter to isolate orange rust or yellow staining and oxidation. Reduce the opacity or turn the masks off to compare the marked region with the original photo and the underlying model.

03

Keep the original observation within reach

Select a mapped finding to see its source photograph, mask, component, recorded grade and analyst note. Photo view uses the source camera pose and lens; Detail frames the region for closer spatial review.

04

Move from the part to the whole

Pull back to the complete stack to understand a finding's height and relationship to the asset. The 78 mapped findings in this dataset are graded Light or Moderate. Mask colours describe visual classes, not measured metal loss.
