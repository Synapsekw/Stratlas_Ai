# The Quarter mark: design

Date: 11 Oct 2026. Status: design approved by the founder (cut A1, motions M1, M3 and M4); the plan is `2026-10-11-quarter-mark.md`.

## Why

The founder was not happy with the Time steps icon (four isometric plates). Two rounds of options were shown as boards, the second one with motion: four pieces take the shape of things the app shows, then lock into the mark. The founder chose **Quarter** and then accepted the recommended cut and motions. The name, the outlined Archivo Expanded wordmark, the Mission colours and the tagline do not change.

## The mark

Three quarters of a ring and a mint square: three dimensions of space and a fourth that is different. It reads as a Q.

Geometry, on the kit's 64 unit grid (cut A1, "Original"):

| Part                    | Value                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Ring centre             | (29, 29)                                                                                         |
| Ring radii              | inner 10.5, outer 23                                                                             |
| Quarters                | north-west, north-east, south-west; each pushed 2 units from the centre on both axes (gaps of 4) |
| Tail (the fourth piece) | a square, 21 wide, from (35, 35) to (56, 56)                                                     |
| Corners                 | every corner rounded with radius 1.6                                                             |
| Bounds                  | 4 to 56 on both axes (52 square)                                                                 |
| Colours                 | the three quarters take the foreground (ink), the tail takes the accent (mint)                   |

Small cut, for 32 px and less: the three quarters close into one piece (a ring section from 90 to 360 degrees, centre (28.5, 28.5), radii 8.5 and 24.5, corner radius 1.2) and the tail moves to (36, 36). The gaps between quarters are under a pixel at 16 px, so they are removed rather than left to blur.

One function in `@aio/brand` holds this geometry. The app symbol, the kit, the app icon and the motion all draw from it, so a later change of proportions is one edit.

## Motion

Four pieces, always the same four. The fourth is always mint.

| Name          | What happens                                                                                                                                | Length | Where                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Step out      | The ring stands closed (four quarters), then the fourth steps out and becomes the square.                                                   | 1.0 s  | Launch screen intro                          |
| Quarter turns | The mark turns a quarter at a time around the ring centre; the square orbits the ring.                                                      | loops  | Loader: a project opening, the globe loading |
| Full sequence | The pieces form a map (four tiles), a model (a cube and a lifted plate), survey dates (a row), the globe (a quartered disc), then the mark. | 4.9 s  | Settings, About: once each time it opens     |

The Short motion from the board (map, globe, mark) is not built: nothing in the app needs it yet.

Rules:

- Reduced motion (Windows, or Settings, Appearance): no motion at all. The still mark is shown; the loader does not turn.
- Motion stops while the window is hidden.
- The launch screen keeps its pointer response: the pieces lean toward the pointer on springs, the tail furthest, and the ring opens a little as the pointer leaves the centre. Idle for 4 s returns them to the centre. Fine pointers only, as today.
- After the intro the tail glows faintly, as the top plate did.

## What changes

1. `@aio/brand`: the geometry, `SYMBOL` (four pieces and the small cut instead of four plates), `icon.svg`, `icon-small.svg`, `mark.svg`.
2. The brand kit (`docs/brand/quadrion/kit/`): symbol, lockups, app icon, PNGs, ICO, favicon and the brand sheet, regenerated by `docs/brand/quadrion/build/kit.mjs`. The kit script also writes the three SVGs in `packages/brand/`, so they cannot drift.
3. Installer and Store images, regenerated by `pnpm icons` (`apps/desktop/build/`, `docs/release/store-listing/images/`).
4. The app: `BrandSymbol` (title bar, About, printed guide) draws the new mark; a new `BrandMotion` plays the motions; the launch screen, the project card's "Opening" state, the globe's loading state and About use it.
5. Text: `packages/brand/README.md`, the launch screen and rename stages in `docs/TESTING.md`.

## What does not change

- `brand.json`, the app id, the Store identity, URL schemes. No `@aio/schema` change, so no contract change.
- The wordmark outlines, the colours, the tagline, the launch screen layout and its timings (intro 1.3 s, exit 0.3 s).
- The Jobs screen (it is being redesigned in another session). Small inline spinners elsewhere keep the plain ring.
- The earlier boards and the launch screen prototype under `docs/brand/quadrion/` stay as the record of round one.

## Risks

- The mark is drawn twice: as exact outlines (still) and as polygons with a round stroke (moving). They must match at rest. A test compares the two outlines.
- Screenshots in the user guide that show the title bar keep the old mark until they are next retaken. Noted as a follow-up, not part of this branch.
- A new app icon does not replace Windows' cached icon for pinned shortcuts until the icon cache refreshes. This is normal for an icon change and is mentioned in the test steps.
