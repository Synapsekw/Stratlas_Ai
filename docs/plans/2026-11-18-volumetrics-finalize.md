# Volumetrics: finalization plan

Date: 18 Nov 2026. Branch `feat/volumetrics-finalize`. Status: approved by the founder on 18 Nov 2026 with the five answers in section 6; the "Do now" items are being built on this branch.

## Why this plan exists

On 18 Nov 2026 the founder declared a feature freeze. From now on one section of the product is finalized at a time, the founder tests all of it, approves it, and only then does work move on. The order is volumetrics, telecom, confined spaces, vertical asset inspection.

"Finalize" means: what exists works reliably, is easy to understand, has no visible loose ends, and can be tested from one list. It does not mean new capability.

Two rules shape every decision below:

- **No new capability.** Anything that adds a feature is deferred unless volumetrics cannot be used without it.
- **Follow the proven volume tools** (founder, 25 Oct 2026; ADR 0013): the person places the boundary and picks the base. The app adds no heuristics of its own.

How this plan was made: the notes, plans, ADR, guide and known limits were read, and each claim was then checked in the code. The punch list says for each item whether the code was checked. Paths starting with `R/` mean `apps/desktop/src/renderer/`.

## 1. What volumetrics is today

### The journey, from data to a report

| #   | Step                       | Where it lives                                                                                                           | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Bring in the surveys       | **New project**, type **Survey**, **Survey data** page; later dates through **Add survey date**                          | Each survey date has a height model (DSM) or point cloud, and usually an orthophoto. The wizard starts the **Volumetric survey** job, which builds the terrain and looks for piles (first two dates only). |
| 2   | Surfaces get ready         | A "Preparing survey surfaces" card on the view, the left sidebar's date rows, the **Processing** drawer                  | Each date's surface is prepared in the background when the project opens (Settings switch: "Prepare survey surfaces when a project opens").                                                                |
| 3   | Mark the piles             | **Tools > Measure > Volume** (draw the boundary at the toe), or **Detect piles** in the empty Volumes list               | Every pile is a volume measurement. It shows in the left sidebar under **Stockpiles**.                                                                                                                     |
| 4   | Pick the base              | The pile panel's **Base** chip (seven bases; **Custom** edits a height per corner)                                       | The figure is recomputed and saved. A "± across bases" hint shows how much the choice of base matters.                                                                                                     |
| 5   | Read one pile              | Click a pile in 3D, on the Map, in the left sidebar or in the inventory: the **pile panel** (Overview, Profile, History) | Volume, tonnes, change since the previous survey, a profile along the pile's long axis, one row per survey date. In 3D the open pile is red over a blue base in a glass box.                               |
| 6   | Read the site              | Right panel, **Project > Volumes**: **Inventory**, **Cut & fill**, **Materials**                                         | Totals by material for the date in focus, a "Not counted" list, cut and fill between two surveys, densities that can change from a survey date.                                                            |
| 7   | Move through time          | The **dates strip** under the view (Volume, Tonnes, Cut & fill, playhead, Compare band, Play)                            | Every figure follows the date in focus. Play crossfades the photos on the Map and swaps 3D halfway. Nothing is estimated between surveys.                                                                  |
| 8   | Change what the view shows | Right panel, **Layers and display > Surface and piles** (Photo, Elevation, Cut & fill, Outlines, Labels)                 | Colours the terrain and shows or hides pile outlines and labels.                                                                                                                                           |
| 9   | Compare one area           | Right-click a polygon, **Compare with previous survey**, tick **Include volumes**                                        | One layer row with the cut and fill heat map inside the polygon.                                                                                                                                           |
| 10  | Export figures             | **Export** in the Volumes header, the Cut & fill pane, the pile's History, Materials, and the **Exports** screen         | CSV files (volume over time, cut and fill, materials, stockpile inventory), and single measurement CSV, DXF and PNG.                                                                                       |
| 11  | Make the customer report   | **Reports > Customer reports > Open volumetric report**                                                                  | An editor with a live preview: Take pictures, edit the texts, switch pages on and off, **Export PDF** and **Export HTML** (one offline file).                                                              |

The numbers come from one place: the survey engine (`packages/survey`, with a Python twin in `python/src/aio_pipelines/survey`). The older "stockpile kit" (`packages/volumetric`, `python/src/aio_pipelines/volumetric`) now only detects piles and draws pictures; it computes no figure the user sees (ADR 0013).

### The same thing done two ways

1. **Volumes sits in two right-panel tabs.** **Project > Volumes** is the inventory. **Tools > Volumes** holds a **Table** tile whose only job is to open Project > Volumes, plus **Swipe**, **Section**, **Bodies** and a second pair of cut and fill From and To pickers (`R/tools/registry.ts:556`, `R/workspace/island/groups.tsx:268`, `R/workspace/workbench/tabsModel.ts:264,315`).
2. **About seven ways to reach a pile or start a volume:** the stage island's Volumes mode, Tools > Volumes > Table, Project > Volumes, Tools > Measure > Volume, the left sidebar's Stockpiles group, a click on a pile in 3D or on the Map, and the command palette ("Volumes: ..."). An eighth, a Stockpiles group in the right panel's "In this project" list, is half wired and never shows (`R/workspace/workbench/project/projectGroups.ts:39`, no entry in `groupRegistry.ts:35`).
3. **The pile panel is mounted in two places:** in **Selection** (`R/workspace/workbench/FrontBody.tsx:94`) and inside **Project > Volumes** (`R/workspace/workbench/benches/VolumesBench.tsx:79`). Which one opens depends on where the click was.
4. **Two cards for one volume:** the general measurement card (`R/survey/card/VolumeDetail.tsx`) and the pile panel (`R/survey/pile/`).
5. **Three charts of volume over time:** the measurement card ("Volume over time"), the pile's History ("Volume and tonnes over time") and the dates strip.
6. **Four ways to see cut and fill between two surveys:** Project > Volumes > Cut & fill; Layers and display > Surface and piles > Cut & fill; Tools > Measure > Site data > **Whole site cut and fill** (an older dialog that runs a job and drafts regions, `R/survey/WholeSite.tsx`); and the area compare on a polygon.
7. **Two base pickers with different words:** the pile panel's picker and the one in "Several measurements at once" (`R/survey/BulkTotals.tsx:477`).
8. **Two reports print stockpiles:** the volumetric customer report and the general survey report (its "Stockpile inventory" and "Volume over time" parts).
9. **Two pile pictures in 3D in older projects:** the new red-over-blue pile look, and the kit's **Volume bodies** (Lifted, In place, Hidden), which stand on the old kit's base "as a picture only" (`docs/KNOWN-LIMITS.md`, Volumes).
10. **Two swipes:** Tools > Volumes > Swipe (older projects only, first and last survey) and the Swipe in the timelapse player.

**Five things are called "section":**

1. **Cross-section**: the Measure tool that cuts a profile along a line.
2. **Section** (Tools > Volumes): the kit's section line between two surveys, older projects only (`R/workspace/VolumeSection.tsx`).
3. **Section plane**: the cutaway that slices the 3D view.
4. **Section view**: the larger chart window, reached by "Open in section view" on one card and "Open larger" on another.
5. **Sections** of the right panel and of a report (the foldable parts).
