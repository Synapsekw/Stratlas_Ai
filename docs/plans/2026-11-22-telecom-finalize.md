# Telecom: finalization plan

Date: 22 Nov 2026. Branch `feat/telecom-finalize`. Status: the build is under way (started 22 Nov 2026); the eight open calls of section 6 were decided on 22 Nov 2026 under the founder's delegation.

**The build started on 22 Nov 2026 without waiting for a founder test of volumetrics.** The freeze rule of 18 Nov 2026 (one section is tested and approved before the next begins) was written for a founder who tests each section himself. On 22 Nov 2026 the founder delegated both the choices and the testing ("pick whatever is best for our project", "you test what you can, don't ask me to test if you can do it"), so the volumetrics verification is driven by an agent in parallel and the main session decided that this build starts now.

## Why this plan exists

On 18 Nov 2026 the founder declared a feature freeze. One section of the product is finalized at a time, the founder tests all of it, approves it, and only then does work move on. The order is volumetrics, telecom, confined spaces, vertical asset inspection. Volumetrics is done (installer 0.32.0). Telecom is next.

"Finalize" means: what exists works reliably, is easy to understand, has no visible loose ends, and can be tested from one list. It does not mean new capability.

Rules that shape every decision below:

- **No new capability.** Anything that adds a feature is deferred unless telecom cannot be used without it.
- **One home for each list and tool, one name per thing.** The founder's pick of 24 Oct 2026 stands: the lists live in the right panel's **Project** tab ("In this project": Antennas, Sectors, Coverage plan), the tools stay in **Tools**, and no tool is hidden behind a menu.
- **A measurement is never guessed.** AI labels a type and proposes a plan; it never writes a measured number (the rule of the antenna and coverage designs).
- **The contracts are frozen.** `packages/schema/src/antennas.ts` and `rf.ts` change only with a row in `docs/architecture/contract-changes.md`. This plan needs none by default (section 4).

How this plan was made: the session notes, plans, guide, known limits and north star were read, five read-only audits ran in parallel (antennas journey, coverage journey, names, back end and packs, notes and tests), and the important claims were then checked in the code by hand. The punch list says for each item whether the code was checked. Nothing was run: no app, no test, no pipeline.

Path shorthand: `R/` is `apps/desktop/src/renderer/`, `M/` is `apps/desktop/src/main/`, `en/` is `packages/ui/src/i18n/en/`, `py/` is `python/src/aio_pipelines/`.

## 1. What telecom is today

Telecom is two halves that share one file. The **antennas** half finds and measures the units on a tower (`telecom/antennas.json`). The **Coverage** half plans sectors from the accepted antennas and computes a coverage map (`rf/sectors.json`, `rf/runs/`). A third, older part is the three hand **measuring tools** (azimuth, tilt, clearance).

### The same thing done two ways

1. **Antennas are listed in one place and counted in two more.** The full list is the Antennas group. Coverage step 1 (`R/rf/panel/StepAntennas.tsx`) holds only counts and a link back to the list, and Coverage step 2 lists the same units again from its own read of `antennas.json` (`M/rf.ts:182`, `RfBench.tsx:635`). The sizing pass counted three lists; there is one list and two views of it.
2. **Azimuth and downtilt can be typed in two places** in the antenna list (the row cell and "Your values" in the details), and are read-only in Coverage (`RfBench.tsx:716-750`).
3. **Accept and Reject behave three ways.** The details button and the A and R keys put a unit back to Draft on a second press; the bulk bar always sets the status; the right-click menu goes back to Draft only when every chosen unit already has that status (`R/telecom/antennaStore.ts:1014`, `AntennaBulkBar.tsx:33`, `antennaActions.tsx:52`).
4. **Sectors are listed twice, from two states.** The Sectors group lists the saved plan; Coverage step 2 lists the working copy. Each has its own rename, and the Project one saves at once (`R/rf/rfStore.ts:553`, `RfBench.tsx:343`).
5. **A cell export is read two ways with two sets of matching rules:** "Use cell export" in the AI card (`R/rf/autoplan/exportMatch.ts`) and the CSV import under Advanced (`R/rf/cellCsv.ts`).
6. **Two buttons start the same run:** "Run coverage" and "Save as new run" call the same function (`RfBench.tsx:1384-1407`). The stage chip has a third Run.
7. **The antenna list has an empty state that names a button it does not have.** Detect antennas is only in Tools; the empty Antennas group says "Detect antennas finds and measures every antenna" with no button (`AntennaInventory.tsx:126-139`).
8. **Messages show away from the action.** A failed delete, save or flag writes a message that only the Detect antennas block in Tools shows (`R/survey/DetectAntennas.tsx:133`).
9. **Two antenna pictures of one tower:** the inventory's boxes, and the engineering model's own approximate antennas with other names (`packages/modelling/src/engineer/assemble/telecom.ts:14`, known limit).
10. **Three hand tools and one detector measure the same angles** with different definitions: the tilt tool reports an unsigned lean from vertical, the detector a signed downtilt.
