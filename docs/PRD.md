## 1. Summary

Quadrion AI is one offline-first Windows desktop app for reviewing, surveying and modelling construction and asset sites from drone and survey data.

Synapse delivers drone and reality-capture work (inspections, surveys, digital twins) as interactive review pages. Six of them proved the format: a chemical storage tank inspection, a flare inspection, a residential development tower facade, a stockpile review, a road project survey and a refinery plant model. Each was a separate web page with its own viewer code, bound to one dataset, hosted on claude.ai, and limited to 256 MB.

The app opens, fuses and reviews these datasets offline, and lets the team build new deliverables from raw data: maps and models from drone photos, survey measurements and volumes, 360 walks, drone panoramas, engineering models, antenna inventories and RF coverage. Its defining capability is **fusion**: any mix of 3D models, point clouds, maps, orthomosaics, drone video with flight logs, photos, 360s and findings shown in one georeferenced scene on one timeline. AI agents from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google are available in every window when the user allows cloud AI.

## 2. Problem

- Each deliverable is a one-off. New jobs mean rebuilding or re-skinning a viewer, and four separate viewer codebases have drifted apart.
- Web hosting caps size and fidelity: photos are downsized, point clouds are thinned to about 1%, ortho pyramids are cut, and data is base64-encoded (33% heavier).
- Customers in oil and gas, utilities and government often cannot use cloud links on site or on restricted networks.
- The ability to make a deliverable lives in scripts and in hand work by Claude, not in a tool the team can run.
- Combining datasets (video on the model, model on the map, two survey dates) is possible only where a viewer was hand-built for it.

## 3. Goals and non-goals

### Goals

Changed 19 Oct 2026 (v1.2): the goals follow the north star (`docs/NORTH-STAR.md` section 1) rather than "replicate the six legacy review pages", which is now one goal among several.

1. Be one offline-first Windows desktop app for reviewing, surveying and modelling construction and asset sites from drone and survey data.
2. Fuse heterogeneous spatial data in one scene and timeline: video projected on models and ground, flight paths, models on maps, point clouds, photos and 360s with poses, findings, across survey dates.
3. Build deliverables from raw data inside the app: maps and models from drone photos, survey measurements and volumes, inspections with reviewed detections, engineering models, antenna inventories and RF coverage.
4. Work fully offline by default, including street maps for the places where projects are; online maps and cloud AI only when a person switches them on.
5. Offer AI agents in every window, with provider and model chosen in Settings and the user's keys stored securely; AI output is always a draft a person approves.
6. Keep opening the existing review packages offline with the same look, behaviour and performance as before, or better.
7. Give customers a read-only player and exportable packages.
8. Be a professionally engineered codebase: typed, linted, formatted, tested, documented, reproducible builds.
9. Stay in internal testing until the founder says otherwise.

### Non-goals (for now)

- LiDAR processing from raw scans (registration, trajectory and SLAM processing stay upstream). Changed 7 Oct 2026 (M10 decision 10, founder: go with the recommendations): photogrammetry from drone images moved into scope (section 9.1); it was listed here with LiDAR as "Photogrammetry or LiDAR processing from raw images or scans (Pix4D, Metashape, DJI Terra stay upstream)".
- Flight planning or drone control.
- A cloud SaaS or multi-tenant server. Optional sync can come later.
- Mobile apps.
- Real-time collaboration.
- Machine telematics (machine tracking), GNSS hardware (ground control targets, base stations, a corrections network) and AI design generation. Stated 7 Oct 2026 (M11 decision 10): surveying measures and checks designs made elsewhere; it does not track machines, supply or drive GNSS hardware, or generate designs.
- macOS builds while it is parked (10 Oct 2026); the codebase is kept portable.

## 5. Releases

Status, 19 Oct 2026: Release A and most of Release B are built and in internal testing. M10 (section 9.1) and M11 (section 9.2) are integrated; M10 founder sign-off is pending. The areas built since v1.1 are in section 9.3. M12 (licensing) comes last, by founder decision.

### Release A: Player and Workspace (build first)

An offline desktop app that:

- keeps a **project library** and opens the six existing jobs and any future offline package built by the existing kits;
- hosts the existing viewers inside one shell with a shared look, navigation, settings and file handling (legacy viewer mode);
- provides the new **Fusion workspace** with the two mandatory experiences rebuilt on the shared engine: a plant model workspace (plant model + map + ortho + video with flight path and ground footprint + point cloud + panoramas) and a tank inspection workspace (tank mesh + LiDAR point cloud + video projected on the mesh + flight scrubber + section cut + findings);
- includes a full **annotation suite** with severity models: issues marked on meshes, images, video, point clouds and maps, linked across datasets;
- ships **offline maps** (street maps where projects are, plus a world overview) and a map pack manager;
- includes **AI settings** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini keys, model routing, offline-only switch) and the **agent panel** framework attached to every window, with a first set of read and navigation tools;
- installs on Windows x64 with a signed installer (macOS parked since 10 Oct 2026).

### Release B: Builder

Authoring inside the app: import raw data, align and georeference, run the Asset Inspection Kit, Volumetric Survey Kit, road and plant pipelines from a UI, human-in-the-loop detection review, AI-assisted detection and narrative, PDF and package export, customer package with read-only player. See section 9.

### Later

- 3D model building from 2D drawings and plot plans in general. Changed 19 Oct 2026: engineering models of chimneys, flare stacks, lattice towers and monopoles from clouds, models, photos and drawings shipped (section 9.3, ENG).
- Change detection across all layer types. Imagery, surface, cloud and model change between dates already exist (guide sections 14 to 16).
- Optional team sync and project sharing.

## 12. Glossary

- **Fusion**: showing several georeferenced datasets in one scene and timeline, with their relationships (video on model, model on map).
- **Package**: a folder or file holding one project's data and manifest, openable offline.
- **Legacy viewer**: an existing kit viewer hosted unchanged inside the app.
- **Flight log**: drone pose over time (position, attitude, gimbal), used to sync and project video.
- **f-theta**: fisheye lens model where image radius is proportional to angle (confined-space drone cameras).
- **COPC**: Cloud Optimized Point Cloud, a LAZ file organised for streaming.
- **PMTiles**: single-file tile archive for offline maps.
- **Walk**: a 360 video recorded on foot, placed on the site with a path.
- **Line-up**: a correction that moves one survey date onto another at read time, without rewriting files.
- **Workbench**: the right sidebar's tabs of tool sections; the **island** is the floating tool bar on the stage.
