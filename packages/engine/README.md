# @aio/engine

three.js scene, cameras, picking, layer adapters, projector shaders, clipping, measure.

See `docs/architecture/SPEC.md` section 2 for ownership and dependencies. Public API: `src/index.ts`.

## Seams for other streams

- Callouts: `EngineStage.setLabelMode('off' | 'key' | 'all')` (selection and hover always; `key` is one callout per tag `area`), `setLabelKeepOut(() => rects)` for UI drawn over the stage (dots, leaders and plates stay out of those client rects), `addLabelObstacles(() => points)` for world points plates must not cover (issue pins).
- Views: `saveView()` / `restoreView(view, animate?)`; a restored view stops the load-time framing. `sectionOrigin()` is the point section offsets are measured from.
- `photos` layers: the engine adapter draws posed photos as frustums (merged outlines, a ghost through walls, instanced image planes) and selects `{ kind: 'photo', id, layer }` on click. Frustum depth follows the photo spacing but is at least 1/120 of the set's extent (drone bursts shot from one hover point are centimetres apart). Where stations (photos within one frustum depth) sit more than 8 depths apart, as with a few photos over a site, each station also gets a fixed-size pin with a stem to the ground, like panoramas; photos with `pos` but no `q` always get one. A pin click selects the station's first photo.
- `panoramas` layers: a marker per panorama with a stem to the ground; a click selects `{ kind: 'pano', id, layer }` and enters the immersive view (camera at the panorama inside a textured sphere, drag to look, wheel to zoom, Esc or Back to return to the saved view). Partial coverage of wide panoramas comes from the project's `panoramas/panoramas.json` (`hspanDeg`, `vtopDeg`, `vbotDeg`), else 2:1 images are full spheres. The HUD keeps below `uiKeepOut()`, the rects given to `setLabelKeepOut`.
- Raster `role: 'plan'` layers are line art with alpha: alpha blended, no depth write, no projected video.
- `SceneHandle.raycast(ndcX, ndcY)`: meshes and rasters, plus every `addRaycastProvider` picker (point clouds), nearest first, then the ground plane y = 0. Section planes hide what they cut.
- `SceneHandle.raycastRay(origin, dir)`: a world ray (photo and frame poses) against visible content, ignoring section planes, then the ground plane.
- Mesh layer roots carry `userData.layerId` (and `userData.aioLayer`).
- `EngineStage.controls` is the OrbitControls (`target`, `enabled`), used by the video rig's follow and drone-eye modes.
- Perf HUD: Ctrl+Shift+F (any build) or `setPerfOverlay(true)` shows fps, frame time p50/p95, points and draw calls of the last frame (every render call, the point-cloud EDL pass included) and a GPU memory estimate (geometry, textures, drawing buffer, shadow map; objects list offscreen scenes they render in `userData.offscreen`). `perfStats()` returns the same numbers for tests; frame times are collected only while the HUD is on.
- Quality: `configureEngine({ quality: { maxPixelRatio, shadowMapSize, shadowSoftness, water, waterFps } })` for new stages and `EngineStage.setQuality` for a live one; the app sets both from its GPU tier presets (`water: 'simple'` and `waterFps: 0` on Low: still water, one normal layer).

## Environment (backdrop, sun, shadows, water)

`EngineStage.environment` and `setEnvironment({ mode, timeMs, water, waterLevel })`; the stage resets to `defaultEnvironment(manifest)` on every project open and the app re-applies the user's per-project choice (`apps/desktop/src/renderer/environment`).

- **Backdrop.** `sky`: three's Preetham `Sky` (scaled by `SKY_GAIN` into the stage's light units, a deep blue floor at night), AgX tone mapping, exposure that adapts at dusk. The sky lights the scene through a PMREM environment map rebuilt only when the sun moves, at most every 150 ms while the time slider is dragged. `studio`: the dark Mission dome with a fixed light and grey image-based light, ACES, as before.
- **Default.** Sky when the project is placed on the Earth (`siteLocation`: a known EPSG CRS and a non-zero origin) and has ground imagery or a basemap (a raster that is not a `plan`); Studio otherwise. A plant on its orthomosaic reads as a place outdoors; a single tank or building model reads better on a neutral backdrop, and a project without a real origin has no sun to place.
- **Sun.** `solarPosition(utcMs, lat, lon)` (NOAA equations, refraction included) at the origin converted to WGS84, turned into the local frame with the CRS grid convergence (`skyDirection`). The default instant is the first flight or timed photo on the latest capture date, else 10:00 site time. Civil time for display is UTC plus `round(lon / 15)` hours (no time zone database offline). Below the horizon a dim moonlight from high opposite the sun's bearing and an ambient floor keep the site readable; `daylight()` derives sun colour (air mass), sky ambient, haze colour and exposure from the same sky model the dome draws.
- **Shadows.** One directional shadow map, fitted to the view (`fitShadow`): centred on the orbit target, sized in steps of the content radius by camera distance and never wider than the content, snapped to whole texels (no swimming while orbiting), depth range tight around the content, normal bias of about a texel. Cascaded shadow maps were considered for Al-Zour (2.6 km): they redraw three maps on every camera move, while the fitted map is redrawn only when the view moves by a few percent and keeps close-ups at a few centimetres per texel. PCF softness per tier. Flat ground pieces (under 1 m tall) and terrain do not cast.
- **Ground imagery** (`groundImageryMaterial`, used for every non-plan raster): the photo as photographed at midday, scaled by the time of day and darkened in the sun's shadow (it already holds the light of its capture, so it is not relit).
- **Water.** Where the project has a sea level (a mesh node `Sea` of type `terrain`: its top) or the user sets one: a level plane with layered scrolling normals from a tileable normal map generated in code (`waterNormalPixels`, nothing fetched), reflecting the sky light map, receiving shadows. Land is kept dry by a land mask (`LandMask`): meshes above the imagery height, then overwritten wherever there is imagery by what the imagery shows (sea is cyan-dominant). Imagery and modelled terrain are cut where the mask says sea, so plot-plan outlines wider than the real shore do not cover the water. Outside the imagery the plane lies under the terrain and land occludes it.
- Point clouds keep their own colours at every time of day (the clouds belong to `@aio/pointcloud`); `environment.night` is there for a tint.
- `SceneView` sets `position: relative` on its root; size it from the host (for example `width: 100%; height: 100%`), not with an absolutely positioned class.
