# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers, surveyors and inspectors preparing and reviewing industrial reality-capture projects on desktop workstations; asset owners receive a read-only player or exported package. This context is derived from docs/design/BRIEF.md and docs/design/DIRECTION.md.

## Product Purpose

Quadrion AI is an offline-first desktop application that brings georeferenced point clouds, models, photos, maps, video and findings into a shared scene and timeline. Engineering modelling must preserve the source location and distinguish measured values from assumptions.

## Brand Personality

Serious, calm and precise. The founder selected the Mission direction: a large fused spatial workspace, collapsible panels and contextual evidence. Use the established app tokens and bundled fonts.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer dashboards, cards everywhere, decorative neon, generic gradients, and mock imagery where real project data is available. Controls and text must not displace the model the user is reviewing.

## Design Principles

- The spatial view and the evidence are central to the task.
- Keep data sharing explicit and local work useful offline.
- Make actions, progress and recovery visible in context.
- Show how a measurement is known and what remains uncertain.
- Preserve the existing Mission interaction and component vocabulary.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Retain existing keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, theme settings and reduced-motion behaviour. Status must be readable without relying on colour alone. No new formal compliance level is claimed by this context document.
